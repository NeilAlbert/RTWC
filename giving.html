Kingdom Giving & Tithes

"Every man according as he purposeth in his heart, so let him give; not grudgingly, or of necessity: for God loveth a cheerful giver." — 2 Corinthians 9:7

Kingdom Investments

Giving Categories

Understand how your financial contributions advance the Gospel and build the body of Christ.

Tithes (10%)

The first tenth of our income returned to the Lord as an act of obedience and worship to support church operations, pastoral care, and holy sanctuary upkeep (Malachi 3:10).

Free-Will Offering

Joyful, voluntary offerings given during weekly services to support local church activities, ministry wings, and administrative overhead.

Building & Development Fund

Dedicated contributions towards constructing, renovating, expanding, and maintaining our sanctuary facilities, audio-visual technology, and parking complex.

Thanksgiving Offering

Special gratitude offerings brought before the Lord in celebration of birthdays, anniversaries, healings, new jobs, or answered prayers.

Seed Offering & Missions

Faith-filled sacrificial seeds earmarked for rural evangelism crusades, foreign missionary sponsorship, and community welfare aid.
