Our History & Legacy

A Beacon of Hope & Pentecostal Power

Royal Temple Worship Centre was established as a vibrant assembly under The Church of Pentecost to serve as a sanctuary of spiritual transformation, fervent prayer, and uncompromised biblical doctrine.

From our humble beginnings with a handful of dedicated believers committed to prayer and fellowship, the Lord has graciously expanded our assembly into a flourishing spiritual home. Today, Royal Temple stands as a central pillar in our district, raising generations of believers who shine as lights in their professions, families, and communities.

As part of the global movement of The Church of Pentecost—which spans over 140 nations worldwide—we align with the overarching vision to possess the nations for Christ by equipping every church member to manifest kingdom values in every sphere of society.

Mission, Vision & Core Values

Guiding our worship, outreach, and community engagement every day.

Our Mission

To unleash the total church to transform their world with values and principles of the Kingdom of God through preaching the uncompromised Gospel, discipling believers, and demonstrating Christ-like compassion.

Our Vision

To be a vibrant, spirit-empowered, and socially impactful worship center where every worshipper experiences God's presence, matures into Christlike authority, and actively fulfills the Great Commission.

Our Core Values

Holy Living, Prayer & Fasting, Biblical Truth, Respect for Authority, Christian Fellowship, Integrity, Compassion, and Excellence in Kingdom Service.

Doctrinal Tenets

The 11 Tenets of The Church of Pentecost

These summaries follow The Church of Pentecost's published tenets. Select one to see a summary and key Scriptures, or read the official statement.

We receive the Holy Scriptures as divinely inspired and authoritative: the final, dependable, and sufficient guide for Christian faith and life.

Key Scriptures: 2 Timothy 3:16–17; 2 Peter 1:20–21

We believe in the one true God, creator of the universe, revealed as Father, Son, and Holy Spirit; one in nature, essence, and attributes, and all-powerful, present everywhere, and all-knowing.

Key Scriptures: Genesis 1:1, 26; Matthew 3:16–17; 28:19; 2 Corinthians 13:14; Psalm 139:7–12

All people have sinned and face the consequences of sin. Humanity needs to repent and be made new by God.

Key Scriptures: Genesis 3:1–19; Romans 3:23; 6:23; Acts 2:38; John 3:3–5

Jesus Christ is the Saviour. We believe in his deity and virgin birth, his atoning death and resurrection, his ascension and continuing intercession, and his return to judge the living and the dead.

Key Scriptures: Matthew 1:21; John 1:1; Matthew 1:18; 1 Corinthians 15:3–4; Acts 1:9–11; Hebrews 7:25

People are called to repent and confess their sins, and are justified through Christ's death. The Holy Spirit sanctifies believers, and God gives them eternal life.

Key Scriptures: Acts 2:38; Acts 3:19; Romans 4:25; 5:1; 1 Corinthians 6:11; Romans 6:23

We practice water baptism by immersion for converts aged 13 and older; infants and children are dedicated to the Lord. The Lord's Supper (Holy Communion) is for members in full fellowship.

Key Scriptures: Matthew 28:19; Mark 16:16; Acts 2:38; Mark 10:13–16; Luke 22:19–20; 1 Corinthians 11:23–33

We believe the Holy Spirit baptizes believers, with speaking in tongues as the initial evidence, and works through spiritual gifts and the fruit of the Spirit.

Key Scriptures: Joel 2:28–29; Acts 2:3–4; Acts 10:44–46; Romans 12:6–8; Galatians 5:22–23; 1 Corinthians 12:8–11

We believe healing is provided for God's people in Christ's atonement. The church also recognizes the use of medication prescribed by qualified medical practitioners.

Key Scriptures: Isaiah 53:4–5; Mark 16:17–18; Acts 10:38; James 5:14–16

We believe in tithes and freewill offerings to advance God's kingdom, and in giving cheerfully.

Key Scriptures: Genesis 14:18–20; Malachi 3:6–10; Acts 20:35; 2 Corinthians 9:1–9

We believe Christ will return and that the dead will be raised: the saved to life and the unsaved to judgment.

Key Scriptures: Daniel 12:2; John 5:28–29; Acts 1:11; Romans 2:7–11

We recognize marriage as a lifelong union ordained by God between a man and a woman, intended for mutual support and family life, including the loving upbringing of children.

Key Scriptures: Genesis 2:18, 21–25; Matthew 19:4–6; 1 Corinthians 7:1–2
