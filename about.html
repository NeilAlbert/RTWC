Our History & Legacy

A Beacon of Hope & Pentecostal Power

Royal Temple Worship Centre was established as a vibrant assembly under The Church of Pentecost to serve as a sanctuary of spiritual transformation, fervent prayer, and uncompromised biblical doctrine.

From our humble beginnings with a handful of dedicated believers committed to prayer and fellowship, the Lord has graciously expanded our assembly into a flourishing spiritual home. Today, Royal Temple stands as a central pillar in our district, raising generations of believers who shine as lights in their professions, families, and communities.

As part of the global movement of The Church of Pentecost—which spans over 140 nations worldwide—we align with the overarching vision to possess the nations for Christ by equipping every church member to manifest kingdom values in every sphere of society.

Doctrinal Tenets

Statement of Faith

The 12 Articles of Faith of The Church of Pentecost. Click any article to expand details.

We believe in the divine inspiration and authority of the Holy Scriptures. The Bible is the infallible, written Word of God, fully trustworthy and our supreme authority in all matters of faith, doctrine, and holy conduct.

We believe in one God, eternally existent in three Persons: God the Father, God the Son (Jesus Christ), and God the Holy Spirit—co-equal in power, glory, and majesty.

We believe that all human beings are born with a sinful nature through the fall of Adam, separated from God, and stand in absolute need of divine redemption through Jesus Christ.

We believe in the virgin birth, sinless life, substitutionary atonement, bodily resurrection, ascension to heaven, and eternal high priesthood of our Lord Jesus Christ.

We believe salvation is received purely by grace through faith in Jesus Christ. Genuine repentance towards God produces spiritual rebirth and justification before God's throne.

We believe in sanctification as a progressive work of the Holy Spirit in the believer, conforming us into the image of Christ and enabling us to walk in practical holiness.

We believe in the baptism of the Holy Spirit for all believers, accompanied by the initial physical sign of speaking in tongues as the Spirit gives utterance, empowering believers for witness and service.

We practice Water Baptism by full immersion in the Name of the Father, Son, and Holy Spirit for born-again believers, and regularly partake in Holy Communion (The Lord's Supper) in remembrance of Christ's sacrifice.

We believe that divine healing is provided in the atonement of Christ and is the privilege of all believers through the prayer of faith and laying on of hands.

We believe in the biblical duty of giving tithes (one-tenth of income) and free-will offerings to support the work of the ministry, global missions, and community welfare.

We believe in the personal, imminent, and glorious return of our Lord Jesus Christ to catch away His Bride (the Church) and establish His righteous kingdom.

We believe in the bodily resurrection of both the saved and the lost—the saved unto eternal life and reward in heaven, and the unsaved unto eternal separation from God.
