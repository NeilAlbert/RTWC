Equipping & Serving

Our Church Ministries

Every believer has a unique calling. Explore our fellowship groups and ministry wings to connect, grow, and serve.

PEMEM

Men's Ministry

Building godly men, faithful husbands, exemplary fathers, and visionary kingdom leaders.

The Pentecost Men's Ministry (PEMEM) caters to the spiritual, emotional, and social development of all adult men. Through monthly breakfasts, leadership retreats, and practical workshops on marriage and financial stewardship, PEMEM equips men to lead with integrity.

Meeting Time: 1st & 3rd Saturday of the month @ 7:00 AM
Leader: Elder Kwame Asante
Women's Movement

Women's Ministry

Nurturing virtuous women of prayer, wisdom, hospitality, and spiritual strength.

The Women's Ministry brings together women of all ages to pray, study the Word, and engage in charitable outreach. Focus areas include home management, mentoring young maidens, maternal welfare, and fervent intercessory prayer.

Meeting Time: Every Tuesday @ 6:00 PM
Leader: Deaconess Grace Addo
PYFM

Youth Ministry

Empowering young people to stand firm in faith, discover their identity, and unleash their talents.

Pentecost Youth Ministry (PYFM) provides a vibrant community for teenagers and young adults (ages 13–30). Activities include youth revivals, career guidance seminars, sports galas, and gospel music sessions.

Meeting Time: Every Sunday @ 4:00 PM
Leader: Bro. Daniel Osei
PENSA

Campus & Student Ministry

Pentecost Students & Associates — discipling tertiary students for academic excellence and soul winning.

PENSA connects university and high school students with local assembly mentorship, rural evangelism missions, and peer discipleship networks during vacations and academic semesters.

Meeting Time: Bi-weekly Saturdays @ 3:00 PM
Leader: Sis. Abigail Baah
Kingdom Kids

Children's Ministry

Instilling biblical foundations in children (ages 2–12) in a fun, safe, and nurturing environment.

Our Children's Ministry runs concurrently with Sunday morning adult worship. Trained teachers guide children through memory verses, puppet shows, Bible storytelling, and creative crafts.

Meeting Time: Sundays @ 8:00 AM (Concurrent Service)
Leader: Deaconess Hannah Appiah
Royal Voices

Choir & Praise Team

Leading the congregation into the glorious presence of God through anointed praise and worship.

The Royal Voices Choir and Music Ministry combine vibrant pentecostal hymns, contemporary gospel praise, and instrumental excellence to usher the sanctuary into high praise and intimate worship.

Rehearsals: Thursdays @ 6:30 PM & Saturdays @ 4:00 PM
Music Director: Bro. Emmanuel Mensah
Soul Winning

Evangelism & Missions

Spearheading personal evangelism, street crusades, prison ministry, and community outreach.

The Evangelism Board leads house-to-house soul winning, hospital visitation, and rural church planting. They provide tract distribution, follow-up for new converts, and baptismal class instruction.

Outreach: 2nd & 4th Saturday @ 9:00 AM
Leader: Elder Kwaku Boateng
Hospitality

Ushering & Protocol

Welcoming worshippers with warmth, maintaining order, and facilitating a seamless service flow.

The Ushering Board creates an inviting atmosphere for visitors and members alike. Responsibilities include seating guests, assisting during offering collection, and supporting sanctuary decorum.

Duty Roster: Sunday & Midweek Services
Leader: Bro. Stephen Kwarteng
Intercession

Prayer Force Ministry

Standing in the gap for the church, national leadership, sick members, and spiritual breakthroughs.

The Prayer Force is the spiritual engine room of Royal Temple. They conduct all-night prayer vigils, early morning prayer hours, and intercede for specific prayer requests submitted by church members.

Meeting Time: Friday All-Night @ 10:00 PM
Leader: Elder Isaac Owusu
