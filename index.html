Welcome Home

A Place of Worship, Faith & Community

At Royal Temple Worship Centre, we are dedicated to proclaiming the uncompromised Gospel of Jesus Christ. As a local assembly under The Church of Pentecost, our mission is to equip believers, foster genuine Christian fellowship, and impact our surrounding communities through love and service.

Whether you are searching for answers, seeking a church home, or looking to deepen your relationship with God, there is a seat reserved for you here.
