Our Ministries

These are the ministries of the assembly. To join one, speak to its leader after a service or contact the church office.

  1. Men's Ministry (PEMEM) The fellowship for the men of the assembly.
  2. Women's Ministry The fellowship for the women of the assembly.
  3. Youth Ministry For the teenagers and young adults of the assembly.
  4. PENSA (Pentecost Students and Associates) For students in secondary and tertiary schools.
  5. Children's Ministry Sunday school and Bible teaching for the children of the assembly.
  6. Choir and Praise Team Leads the singing at our services.
  7. Evangelism Ministry Leads the assembly's outreach and follows up with new converts.
  8. Ushers Welcome people to our services and help them find a seat.
  9. Prayer Force Meets to pray for the assembly and for prayer requests sent to the church.
